## 🎯 The commercial thesis in four lines

1. Second-hand gadgets trade heavily in Zimbabwe, and swapping with a cash top-up is a real, repeatedly requested transaction.
2. Ordinary users will not pay, because Facebook is free. They are the supply and the demand.
3. Dealers will pay, because a qualified lead with a trade-in attached is worth money to them.
4. The feature that attracts users and the product dealers pay for are the same object: a swap post.

---

## ⚠️ The honest position

This is a small-market, low-take-rate business as currently scoped. Revenue comes from dealer subscriptions and promotion, not from transaction fees, because no transactions pass through the platform.

The realistic good outcome for the first version is a modest, profitable niche business. Paths to something larger exist — more categories, more countries, owning the transaction, device financing — but every one of them runs *through* this version, not around it. They are recorded in [../product/roadmap.md](../product/roadmap.md) and [../reference/decisions-log.md](../reference/decisions-log.md) so they are not forgotten and not prematurely built.
